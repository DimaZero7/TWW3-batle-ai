# Sieges and settlement battles

[← Back](README.md) · [Documentation](../../README.md) › [Game knowledge](../README.md) › [Battle mechanics](README.md) › Sieges · [Русский](../../../ru/game/mechanics/sieges.md)

Lower priority for us (we train on field battles). Conventions: [index](README.md). Ours: not modelled.

- **WH3 sieges work like survival battles.** Holding capture points earns the defender supplies to
  build barricades, towers and traps; when the attacker takes a point, the defences built around it
  are destroyed. Taking the victory point starts a countdown to the attacker's win unless the
  defender retakes it. · [fandom Siege battles][fw-siege] · medium-high.
- **4.0:** victory tickets only from the major point; the AI defender spends all funds on
  deployables and stands on barricades; the attacking AI attacks blocking deployables and assaults
  sooner. **4.1.0:** the major point stays locked until the key building point falls; built towers
  reveal stalkers; more barricade slots; wall and tower health grows with wall level (outer walls ~×2);
  at Easy/Normal the attacking AI no longer waits outside for missiles to kill towers. · fandom 4.0,
  4.1.0 · high.
- **Siege beta (July–August 2025):** tower range 350 → 200; level 1–2 towers 6 projectiles a volley
  (was 4); pocket ladders removed (option), buildable ladders; gate/wall health +10/18/24 % by tier;
  attacking AI limited to 4 groups; 50+ siege AI fixes. Which live patch shipped them: not
  confirmed. · [CA thread][beta] · high (beta values).
- **Sally-outs:** the defender sallies at ≥ 1.5× the attacker's strength (towers count ~1000 each)
  or when sitting under enemy missiles is too costly. · twwstats · high.
- **6.1:** when defending reinforcements are destroyed, the garrison no longer routs without a fight. · CA blog · high.
- **Minor settlements** play like field battles; winning captures the settlement. **Survival
  battles:** hold points against waves; supplies from points and damage dealt; a summoned unit costs
  its gold cost in supplies; retreating units teleport out after 10 s. · fandom · medium.
- Flyers can't capture points; "Always Flying" units can't melee gates, walls or barricades (5.0). · fandom · high.
