# Patch history of battle mechanics

[← Back](README.md) · [Documentation](../../README.md) › [Game knowledge](../README.md) › [Battle mechanics](README.md) › Patch history · [Русский](../../../ru/game/mechanics/patches.md)

What WH3 patches changed in the battle mechanics of the other pages, oldest first. Our game is
v9.0.1 (build 50381); the twwstats copy of the key-value tables is 9.0.0 and matches our
`game_rules.json`. Sources: CA patch notes (blogs linked on each page) and the fandom pages
`Update_X`; confidence high unless marked. Conventions: [index](README.md).

**No WH3 patch found that changed** the hit-chance formula (35 / 8 / 90), the armour roll, the
flank/rear defence multipliers (0.6 / 0.3), the charge decay (13 s), the morale tables or the
fatigue thresholds and penalties.

| Patch | Date | Change |
|---|---|---|
| WH2 1.12.1 | 09.2021 | Base of WH3 cavalry: charge bonus out of collision damage; blocked chargers make no charge attack; bracing max at 8 ranks ×4 (was 6, ×3.5), charge defence ×2; knockdown get-up 1–2.7 s |
| 1.1 | 03.2022 | Charge Reflection requires bracing; defenders use their own mass for impact |
| 1.2.0 | 2022 | Unbreakable units no longer rout when all victory points are taken |
| 1.3.0 / 1.3.1 | 06–07.2022 | Overlapping friends counted as one for line of fire; reverted (stacking exploit) |
| 2.0 | 08.2022 | Corpse collision removed; mass changes; wounds/healing reworked for single entities |
| 2.3.0 | 11.2022 | AI evasion and missile targeting by difficulty (Normal: dodges spells only, shoots the first enemy) |
| 3.1.0 | 2023 | Knight attribute (ignores routing peasants); some charge bonuses cut; mass tooltip fixed |
| 4.0 | 08.2023 | AI intelligence and AI stat slider separated; siege tickets and AI deployment; reinforcement range unlimited |
| 4.1.0 | 2023 | Units no longer circle a knocked-down single entity; casters revealed when casting; fewer melee units idling under flyers; siege point locking |
| 4.2.0 | 2023 | Glorious Charge; Elemental and Construct attributes; Rampage reworked |
| 5.0.0 | 2024 | Directional Shield and Ballistic Plating; Empire General shield 35 → 55 %; homing projectiles spread over the unit; pursuers no longer limited to 35 %; Spell Mastery on every caster; Steam Tank rework |
| 5.1.0 | 06.2024 | Collision attacks reworked (weapon-strength damage, cooldowns, max targets); Ogres knocked down again; splash caps cut; mass changes |
| 5.2.1 | 2024 | AI targets vulnerable artillery more |
| 5.3 | 10.2024 | Encourage +4 everywhere; chariot mass brackets 1000 / 1500 / 2000 / 3000; more splash cuts; flyer air-to-ground charge fix |
| 6.0.0 | 12.2024 | Splash targets = weapon strength / 100 (hotfixed to single entities); chariot collision 2 / 2; vigour/terrain shown on the card |
| 6.1 / 6.2.2 | 2025 | Garrison no longer routs when defending reinforcements die; foot characters ≥ 40 % knockback ignore; monster masses up (6.1: infantry "stickier" — player report, low) |
| Siege beta | 07–08.2025 | Tower range 200, ladders, AI limited to 4 groups (live patch not confirmed) |
| 7.0 | 12.2025 | Dragons heavier and faster; shooters keep distance and use leftover ammo |
| 7.2 | 2026 | AI doesn't split to chase flyer groups; lords stop flip-flopping |
| 8.0 / 8.1 | 2026 | AI clumping, outflank idling, blocked shooters, edge stacking fixed; bracing only against cavalry heading at the unit; small-size spell scaling fixed |
| 9.0 | 09.2026 | "Sharp stones" speed penalty removed; skirmishing units can trigger rampage; map fixes |
